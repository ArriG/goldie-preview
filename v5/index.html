Straight answers

What practices ask first.

Compliance, built in

  • Only people who enquired with you, consent checked first
  • Every message names your practice and has an opt-out
  • AU Spam Act 2003 · NZ Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007
  • No clinical advice, no discounts, no review incentives

Not legal advice. We go through it together before anything sends.

Won’t this annoy patients?

One friendly text from your practice, about something they asked you about. No reply means a short follow-up at most, then we stop. STOP opts them out straight away, and you see every opt-out.

My front desk has no time.

They don’t chase. We do. They get a patient who wants to talk, with a summary.

Agencies send us junk leads.

We don’t sell leads. Only your own enquirers who say they’re still keen. You pay when they pay.

Do we have to change systems?

No. One export, one sign-off. Bookings go in your own diary, made by your team.

What if nobody goes ahead?

You pay nothing, and you’ve learned what your old list is worth.
