Questions

Fair questions.

What does the pilot cost?

No upfront fee. You pay when a re-engaged patient meets the agreed criteria and pays for treatment. Fee, criteria and attribution are agreed first. Anything after the pilot is scoped separately.

[Confirm how post-pilot work is priced]
Will patients be talking to AI?

AI-assisted SMS may be part of it. We agree the wording, how it’s introduced and when a person steps in.

Will this add work for reception?

No call lists. Your team gets interested patients with a short summary.

Can you message our whole database?

No. We agree which enquiries are suitable and check permissions and exclusions first.

Does it work with our software?

Goldie works alongside your tools. We check compatibility before you commit.

What about clinical or urgent questions?

They go to a person. Goldie gives no clinical advice and doesn’t decide urgency.

Do we have to fix every leak?

No. Start with the pilot. Decide on the next leak once you’ve seen the first one work.
